Response framework

Incident Response Lifecycle

Six phases provide a repeatable structure for investigation and response. Actions should be adapted to the incident, lab scope, and available evidence.

PHASE 01

Preparation

Maintain tools, access, logging, response procedures, and evidence-handling readiness.

PHASE 02

Identification

Validate the alert, identify affected assets, establish scope, and determine initial severity.

PHASE 03

Analysis & Investigation

Correlate events, build a timeline, collect artifacts, and document findings and uncertainty.

PHASE 04

Containment

Limit further activity using authorized, proportionate actions and preserve relevant evidence.

PHASE 05

Eradication & Remediation

Remove the cause, address affected accounts or systems, and close identified control gaps.

PHASE 06

Recovery & Lessons Learned

Validate normal operation, monitor for recurrence, and record improvements and follow-ups.

Operational checklist

Evidence & Analyst Handover

Use these checklists during lab exercises. Checkmarks are local to this page session and are not saved to the incident files.

Evidence Collection Checklist

Analyst Handover Guidance

  1. Summarize what triggered the investigation and the current status.
  2. Identify affected assets, accounts, known scope, severity rationale, and unresolved questions.
  3. Provide a timestamped event timeline and links to supporting evidence.
  4. List containment or remediation actions already taken, including who authorized them.
  5. State the next recommended investigative tasks and any monitoring requirements.
  6. Record the receiving analyst, handover time, and acknowledgment in the incident record.
ⓘ
Lab safety and evidence integrity

Perform testing only on systems and accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. Keep response actions proportionate to the lab scenario. Do not claim containment, eradication, recovery, or incident closure unless the action was performed and its result was verified. Replace planned case details with your actual Wazuh evidence.
