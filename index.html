Operational approach

SOC Investigation Workflow

A simple workflow used to organize lab exercises and document security investigations.

STEP 01

Collect

Generate or collect endpoint events and confirm the relevant agents and log sources.

STEP 02

Detect

Review Wazuh alerts and rule matches, then identify events requiring further investigation.

STEP 03

Investigate

Correlate timestamps, users, processes, and related events to document the findings.

STEP 04

Document

Record evidence, conclusions, and response steps in the relevant incident or testing report.

ⓘ

About this lab

This is a personal learning and demonstration environment. Dashboard images, alert examples, and scenario results should be interpreted as lab evidence only when accompanied by the corresponding configuration, test procedure, and captured results. No live monitoring metrics are claimed on this overview page.
